Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Shanghai Zhihui Tongxin Technology Co., Ltd operates Live Translingo. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app. You can use Live Translingo without creating an account.

Information used to provide the app

  • Text translation. For online translation, we receive the text you submit, the source and target languages, and your selected translation options. If you request an alternative translation, we also send the first translation to help generate different wording. Offline text translation uses downloaded Apple language assets and processes the text on your device.
  • Photos. If you translate a photo you take or choose, we receive a resized copy of the image and the selected languages. This can include text and other details visible in the image. The app uses the system photo picker when you choose an image.
  • Voice. Text dictation and manual voice mode use Apple speech recognition on your device when a supported recognizer is available. Manual voice mode sends the recognized text and language choices for translation. Live and headphone translation stream microphone audio and language choices while you are actively translating.
  • AI tools. If you use a writing or practice tool, we receive the text you submit and any recent turns, practice settings, or other context needed to answer it.
  • Service and security information. We use a pseudonymous installation identifier, Apple App Attest verification data, request identifiers, usage counts, and technical request information such as your IP address to verify legitimate app installations, deliver and protect the service, limit abuse, and diagnose errors.

Service providers and processing locations

With your permission, we send the relevant information to the providers needed for the online feature you use. DeepSeek processes some text translations and AI tool requests. Alibaba Cloud Model Studio processes some text translations, manual voice transcripts, and some photo or live voice requests. NetEase Youdao processes photo translation and live or headphone voice translation. The provider can vary by feature, language pair, and service availability. We disclose the applicable provider or providers before sending your content.

Our gateway is hosted in the United States. Depending on the route, providers may process data in the United States, Singapore, or other jurisdictions where they operate. We require service providers that receive personal information from us to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Storage and retention

We use submitted text, images, and audio to complete your request. Our gateway does not store that content in its usage database. When an image provider needs to fetch a copy, the gateway makes it available through a short-lived link for the request. Service providers may handle or retain information according to the terms that apply to their services.

Request and daily usage records older than the preceding day are cleared during request processing. We retain device verification and operational security records for as long as needed to protect and operate the service or meet applicable legal requirements.

Saved translation history, photo favorites, and learning practice progress are stored on your device. You can delete saved translations and favorites in the app and reset learning practice in the learning tool. You can turn off future history saving in Settings. If you save an image to Photos, that copy is managed by your Photos settings and can be deleted in Photos.

Your choices and requests

To withdraw permission for future online sharing, open Settings and choose Withdraw Online Sharing Permissions. This does not undo processing already performed. You can manage microphone, speech recognition, camera, and photo-saving permissions you granted in your iPhone settings.

You can contact us to ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. We may ask for information needed to locate the relevant records and verify the request. Please avoid including sensitive content in your initial message.
